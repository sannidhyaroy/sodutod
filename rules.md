# ⚡ boot-log

Welcome to **Soduto Labs** — the community around [Soduto](https://github.com/sannidhyaroy/Soduto), the KDE Connect-compatible macOS app. This is a place to hang out, talk, test things, and shape where the app goes. No formality required.

A few things to keep it running smoothly:

1. Be decent to each other. Disagreement is fine; dogpiling isn't.
2. Channels have purposes — support in the support forum, bugs in the bug forum, ideas in the proposals forum. General chatter and truly anything-goes each have their own channel.
3. No spam or sketchy links.
4. Discord's ToS applies here — not our preference, just the reality of being hosted on their platform. That means no piracy links or warez talk *on the server*. What you do off-platform is your own business.
5. When reporting bugs, logs and repro steps go a long way — high-signal reporters tend to get noticed. 📡
